# Phenom

Phenom's apply wizard on employers' careers sites, observed on live postings, last on 2026-10-30. The fixture pages are [tests/pages/phenom/job.html](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/job.html) and [apply.html](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/apply.html), configured by [form_schema.json](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/form_schema.json), [MULTI_PAGE_form_schema.json](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/MULTI_PAGE_form_schema.json), [Messages_en_us.json](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/Messages_en_us.json) and the loaders' answers in [references.json](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/references.json), served as `careers.acme.example` and Phenom's CDN in `tests/test_phenom.py`: a four-step wizard with one example of each rendering below.

## Where the form is

- Each employer has its own host (`careers.<employer>.com`, `jobs.<employer>.com`). A job is `/<site>/<lang>/job/<jobSeqNo>[/<slug>]`, the site a country (`us`, `gb`, `ca`) or `global`. The double-send key is the host and the `jobSeqNo`.
- The job page's Apply link (`data-ph-at-id=apply-link`, `phw-tk=apply_click`) leads to the wizard, `/<site>/<lang>/apply?jobSeqNo=` (`hvhapply` for some hourly roles, the same configuration), or to the system behind Phenom: Workday, Taleo, iCIMS, ADP, Oracle, Ashby. The job page's `phApp.ddo.jobDetail.data.job.externalApply` says which. The backing system does not decide it. A link to another host is `unsupported`, `external_step`.
- The wizard is react-jsonschema-form (`form.rjsf`) with Phenom's own widgets, three to eight steps. Only the current step is in the page. Next (`button#next`) posts the step to `/applySubmit` (`ddoKey: "applySubmit"`, the step's answers under `formData`), which creates a Phenom candidate profile on the first step and checks the step server side; the last step's Submit sends the application to the backing system.
- On a fresh session, one site's wizard first shows its terms (`page=terms`): a privacy notice whose one button, I Accept (the form's submit), leads to the form (`page=applyForm`). Accepting is not kept: each session shows the terms again. Filling accepts them, a declared side effect; a read takes the configuration from the terms page.
- A cookie banner (Phenom's own `[ph-module=gdpr]`, TrustArc, OneTrust, cookieconsent's `.cc-window`) shows at any time over the form; the blocker's lists hide it. A loading overlay (`.overlaybg`) covers the form while a step loads.
- A site's firewall (CloudFront) counts `/applySubmit` posts: past a burst it answers every one `403 Request blocked`, the wizard's own included, for up to an hour while its pages still load (2 sites). A site's first read, its country requests, can pass and get the fill's own posts refused: the wizard shows no refusal, the step stays or says `Please select the country`. A read or fill met with that `403`, on any post, is `blocked`, `rate_limited`, and the next run reads the countries from the cache.
- A closed posting's job URL and wizard URL both answer `404` with Phenom's error page, its `phApp.pageName` `"404"` ([tests/pages/phenom/404.html](https://github.com/shbernal/atsfill/blob/main/tests/pages/phenom/404.html)): `unsupported`, `posting_gone`.

## Reading the form

The apply page's HTML assigns `phApp` (`refNum`, `locale`), `phApp.ddo.response.response` (the backing system as `applyATS`, `jobCountry`, `jobData` with the questionnaires' ids and `has…Questionnaire` flags) and `phApp.sessionParams.csrfToken`. The page's scripts' world is out of the adapter's reach in the browser, so both reads parse this hydration data from the HTML, fetched (`request_read`) or from the loaded page (`browser_read`), and both then read the same way:

- The configuration is public on the CDN: `cdn-prod-static.phenompeople.com/CareerConnectResources/<refNum>/<applyATS, lowercased>/external/careersite/singlepage/schema/form_schema.json`, its labels in `bundles/Messages_<locale>.json` and `Messages_default.json` (`{{key}}` in titles and options).
- One site of 20 runs the multi-page renderer (`MULTI_PAGE_renderer.js` in the apply page). The renderer tries the same path under `multipage/`, which that site lacks (`403`), then takes the form page `<refNum>/schema/apply/MULTI_PAGE_form_schema.json` names (`terms`, then `applyForm`) from `<refNum>/schema/apply/multipage/<schemaname>.json`, its labels from `<refNum>/bundles/apply/`. The site's `singlepage` configuration still answers and is stale: it lists fields the wizard has dropped, `gender` and `ethnicity` for a US job, and a step.
- `schema` and `uiSchema` describe every step's fields; a top-level property's `uiSchema.nav` names its step, a key of `stepConfig` (or its `stepId` or name). A step whose `stepRules` hold on `phApp` (no secondary questionnaire for the job) is not shown.
- A loader (`apiConfig.payload`, `%%phApp.…%%` filled in) posted to `/applySubmit` with the token serves the rest: the job's questionnaires (`asyncfield`: `getWorkdayJSQs` by questionnaire id, SuccessFactors' `getJsq` by job, which also takes `jobSeqNo`, `jobId`, `locale` as `en_US` and an `apTxnId`), each country's fields (`getCountrySpecificFields` by `countryCode`, under `cntryFields.`: one request a country, 245 on one site, eight at a time, kept a day in the cache directory for the site's later reads; a field shows, is required and lists its options under the countries whose fields say so), lists (`asyncselect`, by the uiSchema's `datasourcepath`, `responseField`, `displayTextField`, `valueField`), and a search's first suggestions (`asynctypeaheadv3`, by `ui:options`' `dataFieldMapKey`), listed as its options: some of what it takes, not all. A questionnaire with an empty loader is built by a workflow (`psqToSchema`, Taleo's prescreening) from `jobData.<sourceName>.questions`: each question's id is `<targetName>.questionnaireId.<questionId>`, a follow-up names its parent question and option.
- A list a workflow loads when another answer changes (`workflow`, an `onchange` `http` node on `%%formData.<parent>%%` and a transform to `properties.<list>`) is a dependent list. Each parent value costs a request, so it has one option set, keyed to the parent's answer before any: its `preFillWindowObj` value or `default`, else the job's country where the parent lists it, else its first option. Under any other answer its options are unknown (`options` null) and the read-back decides. A list under two answers (a region under state and country) takes the guess on the one that is itself a dependent list, and its set is keyed on both where the other holds a value before any (`AllOf`). An `asyncselect` whose own loader names an answer (`dependentKey`, an entry's country) depends on it the same way, within its entry. The job's country is a name and a country list's values are codes (`USA`): the phone code list (`USA_1`, `United States of America (+1)`) maps one to the other.
- An entry that only asks to pick one (`Choose one option`) is not an option. The page's own placeholder has no value, but one site's `en_global` bundle gives that text to a source's real value, which `en_us` calls `Agency`.
- Phenom's applicant-source workflow fills its lists from a `temp_data` `jsonData` table: a list of the sources, or the sub-sources keyed by a source's value. The workflow's `params.field` names the source list, so a keyed table is a dependent list with every set in the page data: one option set per source the source list offers, a key it does not offer left out (2 of 2 sites with a table). A `dependent_utm` tree is all in the page data: one option set per "how you heard" answer (`sourceTypeSchema`), a hidden sub-source (`show: false`) left out.
- `allOf` rules add properties when their `if` holds: on the page's own data (a hidden field, `locale`) it is decided at once, a hidden field holding its `preFillWindowObj` value, else the job's data of its name (`jobCountry`), tested by a `const`, an `enum` or Phenom's `matchesAnyOf` (one value, an `anyOf` of them for a country's name and code; an exact match on one site, not seen otherwise), so a question asked only for another country than the job's is not read, or by a `pattern` matched in it (a job location's county); on answers it is the field's `shown_when`, a `const` or `enum` an `Is` on the options' labels, `not` negating it, `anyOf` and `allOf` joining parts, and a resume given (`resumeName` `isNotEmpty`) `Is("resume")`; a field a rule adds keeps the rule's `required`, required whenever shown, and a rule requiring a field already there is its `required_when`. A field asked only without a resume is not read and, where it is required, the resume is. A job question's `parentQuestionId` and `parentOptionId` are its `shown_when`.

A top-level list that holds an answer before any (its `default`, the job's country) has that option's text as the field's `default`, the page's, not the account's, so a required one is still answered; a signature's date (`defaultTodayDate`) is `prefilled` with today's, the only one the page takes; a `removeField` common rule leaves its field out; a `maxLength` is the field's.

A field's id is its schema path, dotted (`phoneWidget.phoneNumber`, `jsqData.QUESTIONNAIRE-3-185.a`, `experienceData[0].fromTo.startDate`), which is the control's DOM id, a radio group's `name`, or a checkbox group's boxes' id without `_<n>`; the resume is `resume`. Required: the parent's `required` list or `ui:required`.

## Filling

- Filling clears the site's storage and cookies and opens the wizard anew. The resume is uploaded first, once its input shows (on one site a moment after the form), and the page's requests left to settle: its parse fills and clears fields when it comes back.
- Each step's answered fields, those the page shows in the configuration's order (a country before its states), are filled as the page renders them; then the step is scanned again, for a field an answer showed (follow-ups three deep), and filled until its read-back holds after two seconds, six rounds at most. A field never shown has no read-back. The wizard keeps only its current step, so the fields shown once filled are those each step showed before Next. A control disabled until another answer waits a round; a text box that became a list (a region, once the state is set) is filled as one.
- A list is set even to the value it holds, cleared first, so a list depending on it loads; a dependent list without the answer is set once more after its parent is set again.
- The page sends a country's request for its fields 0.3 s after the change. Two answers to other fields within that time drop it: the list still shows the country, but the step lacks it and Next answers `Please select the country`. Filling waits after a country until the page's requests have settled.

## Read-back and submit

- The read-back is each step's controls, read before Next: values, a select's text, the checked radio's or boxes' labels, a box as `Yes`/`No`, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` from the picker's placeholder, a national phone with its code.
- On one site, a read's 245 country requests were the burst that got `/applySubmit` refused (see above). A read with the countries kept sends none.
- `/applySubmit` checks some answers against the employer's records: a referral by someone who is not an employee. It answers `status: failure`, `statusType: VALIDATION_ERROR` with a `statusMessage`, and the page stays on the step showing nothing. That answer is not taken.
- On one site, a stylesheet of the employer's hides the preferred last name, label and input, for every country, yet the country fields require it once a preferred name is given: the step does not pass for a person either, and the read, which sees no stylesheet, lists the field. Next's error under a control the page hides is the known gap `hidden_required`.
- On one site, an employer search with `allowNew` reads back its pick, yet the step still finds it empty (`is a required property`) and does not pass.
- Submit is the last step's submit button; the evidence is `/applySubmit`'s answer to the post with `isLastStep: true`. No send has been made through this adapter, so the end of a send is unobserved.
